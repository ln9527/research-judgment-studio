# Data card: human expert and junior researcher ratings (management benchmark)

**Source:** Gong, Li, and Zhou (2026). Public anonymized rating files are in [FutureTech-OB/ai-taste](https://github.com/FutureTech-OB/ai-taste). Ethics approval: Tsinghua University, Project No. THU-04-2026-0034.

**Raters.**
- 48 experts: current editors and editorial board members of leading management journals, recruited by direct one-to-one professional contact (5 assistant, 17 associate, 12 full professors, 12 chaired or endowed professors, 2 not reported). 384 ratings; 8 pitches per expert; mean 3.2 expert ratings per pitch.
- 174 junior researchers: doctoral students, postdoctoral researchers, and early-career faculty recruited through professional networks. Raters averaging under one minute per pitch were excluded. 2,530 ratings; mean 14.5 pitches per rater and 21.1 ratings per pitch.
- Total 2,914 ratings of the 120 held-out management pitches. Junior raters were compensated with 100 RMB and/or access to a research tool. No speed filter was applied to experts.

**Instrument.** Prior exposure to the study; predicted tier on a Top / Top- / Good / Fair scale (mapped to exceptional / strong / fair / limited); confidence (1 to 5); topic familiarity (1 to 5).

**Headline results.** Individual accuracy 36.2% (experts) and 31.7% (juniors); majority-vote accuracy 41.6% (experts, 89 non-tied items) and 40.8% (juniors, 103 non-tied items); chance 25%. Exact-agreement Fleiss kappa 0.047 (experts) and 0.032 (juniors).

**Privacy.** Rater identities and fine-grained combinations of rank, subfield, and institution are not released.
