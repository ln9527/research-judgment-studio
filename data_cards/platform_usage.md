# Data card: Idea Gatekeeper platform (descriptive only)

**What it is.** A public web platform at https://gatekeeper.spansurvey.net that serves the fine-tuned evaluators. Modules visible on 28 October 2026: New Evaluation, Evaluation result, Idea Lab, Idea Hatch, Research Pulse, Taste Arena (screenshots in `platform/screenshots/`).

**Usage counts.** As of 28 October 2026 (Asia/Shanghai), platform records show 1,417 registered users and 3,455 evaluations (3,204 management, 251 economics). These are aggregate counts from platform records. No user-level data, submitted ideas, or account information are released in this repository.

**Not yet built.** No module on the live site implements the multi-round auto-research loop or the proposed doctoral training studio. Those are proposed work.
